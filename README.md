# Beyond the Book

A design provocation: what if the research behind Chris Miller's *Chip War* never became a printed nonfiction book?

Twelve working, single-file prototypes of the same material told as something else — a scroll you fall down, a supply chain you can sever, a policy war you can play, a book you can argue with, a sonification, a stage play, a counterfactual engine, a card game — plus a "Projector" dial that fast-forwards the media tech 18 months.

And a site-wide **Chip War ⟷ Robot research** toggle: flip it and the whole gallery re-skins for the next book — embodied AI — where the chokepoints move from EUV and TSMC to rare-earth magnets, harmonic-drive actuators, and world-model weights.

One `index.html`. Zero dependencies. All figures real and dated; demos illustrative. An homage, not affiliated with the author or publisher.
